# Contributing to the Cyber Master Series — Certification Study Series

Thank you for helping improve the free Certification Study Series created by Taher Amine ELHOUARI.

## What you can report

- Incorrect or outdated certification/exam information
- A changed official exam outline, blueprint, objective, policy, or reference
- Technical inaccuracies
- Ambiguous or confusing explanations
- Broken links
- Accessibility or usability issues
- Typos and formatting problems
- Suggestions for future certification/standards guides

## Source expectations

For factual or exam-related corrections, include a public authoritative source whenever possible. Preferred sources are the certification owner, standards body, official documentation, or another primary source.

## What must not be submitted

Do **not** submit:

- exam dumps or braindumps;
- recalled live-exam questions or answers;
- material covered by a certification body's candidate NDA/confidentiality rules;
- leaked, stolen, or restricted exam content;
- copied pages or substantial passages from copyrighted books, courses, or paid training material;
- proprietary organizational information or confidential client material.

Issues containing such material may be closed or removed.

## How to contribute

This repository is primarily a public documentation and issue-tracking hub. The interactive HTML source and DOCX masters are maintained privately.

1. Search existing issues to avoid duplicates.
2. Open the most appropriate issue template.
3. Identify the guide and edition affected.
4. Explain the problem clearly.
5. Provide the authoritative source or rationale.
6. Do not include protected exam content.

## Editorial control

All suggested changes are reviewed before inclusion. Acceptance of an issue or suggestion does not guarantee publication, and final editorial decisions remain with the project author/maintainer.
